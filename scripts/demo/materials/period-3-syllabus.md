# Year 7 Humanities: course syllabus, 2026–27

Oakfield Middle School (fictional). Period 3 Humanities. Teacher: Ms J. Harrow (fictional).
Every name, date and policy in this document is invented for the Throughline demo.

## What this course is for

By the end of the year, students should be able to:

- Explain how geography shaped where and how the first civilisations grew.
- Read a primary source and say who made it, when, and what it was for.
- Build an argument from evidence, in discussion and in writing, and change their mind when the evidence says so.
- Write a clear paragraph with a claim, two pieces of evidence, and a sentence that ties them together.
- Work in a group so that everyone in it gets to speak.

## How the grade is calculated

| Category | Weight |
|---|---|
| Homework | 20% |
| Quizzes | 20% |
| Projects | 30% |
| Tests | 30% |

Homework is marked for completion and effort. Quizzes are short, every two weeks or so, and the lowest quiz score is dropped at the end of each term. Projects are the big pieces (the timeline, the city-state podcast, the DBQ essay) and come with a rubric. Tests close each unit.

## Units

1. The Fertile Crescent and the first cities (September to mid-October)
2. Hammurabi, law and fairness (mid-October to November)
3. Egypt and the Nile (December to January)
4. Greece: city-states, democracy and the Persian wars (February to March)
5. Rome from republic to empire (April to May)
6. Choice project and exhibition (June)

## Policies

- Late homework is accepted for full credit up to one week late; after that it is half credit until the end of the term.
- Any quiz can be retaken once within two weeks for the higher score.
- A missed test is made up during the next lunch slot; a missed project deadline is discussed with the student first.
- Phones stay in bags during class. First reminder is free; the second means the phone goes on the desk at the front.
- Work handed in must be the student's own; a source used must be named.
- Families can expect an email in the first week of each unit with what we are reading and when the test is.
