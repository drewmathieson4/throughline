# Year 6 Humanities: end-of-year progress reports

Oakfield Middle School (fictional). Class 6H. Teacher: Ms R. Teller (fictional).
Date: 19 June 2026

These reports accompany the summer letter home. Grades use the school scale: Exceeding, Meeting, Approaching, Beginning. Every student and name in this document is invented for the Throughline demo.

## Avery Quill

Avery is a thoughtful reader whose written work shows real care. Her essay on the Nile flood cycle was the clearest in the class. She is reluctant to speak in front of others and asked to be excused from the end-of-year presentation; we agreed she would present to a group of three instead, which she did well. Next year's teacher should know that she will not volunteer, but she has plenty to say when asked directly. Overall: Meeting.

## Rowan Pell

Rowan is enthusiastic, quick, and a constant talker. He argues well and enjoys a debate, and his contributions are usually on topic, but he struggles to let others have a turn. He did his best work this year when seated away from his friends and given a clear job. Written work is rushed and would benefit from a second draft. Overall: Meeting.

## Sable Thorncroft

Sable is a quietly outstanding student. Full marks on every quiz this year, and the timeline project was exhibited in the library. She rarely puts her hand up, so it would be easy to overlook her; she responds well to being asked to explain her thinking to a partner. Overall: Exceeding.

## Marlowe Finchley

Marlowe has had a difficult year with organisation. Homework was frequently missing or left at home, and he came to class without a notebook more often than not. When the work is in front of him he can do it: his map of the Mediterranean trade routes was accurate and well labelled. A folder system was trialled in the spring term with some success. Overall: Approaching.

## Ember Castellane

Ember writes with unusual clarity for her age and reads far beyond the class texts; she asked for the extension reading on three occasions. She is a steadying presence in group work. The one thing to watch is that she can be impatient with slower classmates. Overall: Exceeding.

## Alex Marsh

Alex is capable and generally pleasant, but has become quieter over the course of the year and his written work has thinned out since the spring. Two assignments were handed in late in the final term. Nothing that needs alarm, but a check-in early next year would be worthwhile. Overall: Meeting.
